What to look at

Each circuit has a port that several links attach to.

Nudge shared paths apart splits the links that leave one port into parallel lines. With it off, they share one trunk that branches. Either way they meet on the port.

Nudge link ends apart at a port only acts on links that already run side by side: on its own it changes nothing here. Together with one of the other two options it moves the link ends off the port, beside it.

Nudge touching colinear segments apart on its own changes nothing here either.

The status line counts the link ends that are not attached to the center of their port. Drag an element to route again.

Router setup

Orthogonal routing, shape buffer distance 10, ideal nudging distance 5 and the unifying nudging preprocessing step, as @joint/router-avoid configures libavoid. It turns the first two options on and the third off.

Every element is an obstacle with a pin at its center and one per port. Unlike @joint/router-avoid, there is no fallback for a link libavoid cannot route; every link here can be routed.
